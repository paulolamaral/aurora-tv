About

Aurora TV plays live TV, movies and series from your own IPTV subscription (Xtream Codes login or M3U playlist). The app does not include, provide or sell any content.

O Aurora TV reproduz TV ao vivo, filmes e séries da sua própria assinatura de IPTV (login Xtream Codes ou lista M3U). O app não inclui, não fornece e não vende nenhum conteúdo.

Aurora TV is an independent app and is not affiliated with any content provider. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
