Getting started

On first launch, type a name for your list, paste your provider's server address (or M3U link) and, if you have one, the user name and password. Select "Salvar e entrar". With an M3U link, leave user and password empty.

Na primeira abertura, digite um nome para a lista, cole o endereço do servidor do seu provedor (ou o link M3U) e, se tiver, usuário e senha. Selecione "Salvar e entrar". Com link M3U, deixe usuário e senha em branco.

Common questions

  • The app opens empty. You need your own IPTV subscription or playlist. Aurora TV does not provide content.
  • A channel or video does not play. Check your internet connection and your subscription with your provider. Use "Atualizar catálogo" in Settings to refresh the list.
  • Lock adult content. Settings, Adult content lock, Create PIN.
