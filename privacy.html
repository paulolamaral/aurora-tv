Summary

Aurora TV does not collect, store or sell your personal data on any server of ours. We have no accounts, no analytics and no advertising. Everything the app saves stays on your TV.

Data saved on your TV

To work, the app saves the following on your TV only:

  • The lists you add: name, server address, user name and password (or the M3U link).
  • Your profiles, favorites, watch progress and last watched channels.
  • Your adult content PIN and category preferences, if you set them.
  • An optional TMDB key, if you enter one.
  • A local copy of the catalog, to open faster.

This data is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

Data sent to other services

  • Your IPTV provider. The app connects to the server you configure and sends the login you typed, so it can load your catalog and streams. What that provider collects is governed by its own policy.
  • TMDB (themoviedb.org). To show synopses, posters and cast, the app may send the title you are viewing to TMDB. TMDB receives the TV's IP address and that request. See the TMDB privacy policy.
  • YouTube (Google). When you open a trailer, the app loads the YouTube player. Google receives the TV's IP address and the video requested. See the Google privacy policy.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

Content

Aurora TV has no content of its own. It plays only what comes from the provider you choose. You are responsible for having the right to use that content. Adult categories from a provider can be hidden or locked with a PIN, and the Kids profile hides them.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from them. A Kids profile is available to hide adult categories.

Your choices

You can edit or delete lists, profiles and favorites inside the app, and remove everything by uninstalling it. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export or erase on our side.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
